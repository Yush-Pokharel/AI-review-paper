## Agent Rules
- Section 10 (Challenges): Synthesize actual limitations mentioned across studies (not generic AI challenges). Group by theme: data quality, annotation difficulty, environmental conditions (lighting, water turbidity, weather), model generalization, hardware constraints.
- Section 11 (Future): Based directly on gaps from Section 10. Each future direction should address a specific gap.

For each gap: State problem -> Evidence (which studies mention it?) -> Why it matters -> Proposed solution direction.

- Ensure future directions are actionable (e.g., "Develop underwater-specific augmentation techniques using simulated turbidity" rather than just "more research needed").
