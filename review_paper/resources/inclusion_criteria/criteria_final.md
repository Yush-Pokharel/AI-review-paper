# INCLUSION / EXCLUSION CRITERIA (Updated — Confirmed 2026-09-07)

## Time Range
Early 2015 to early 2026 (a decade of deep learning and computer vision for environmental monitoring).

## Inclusion Criteria (All Must Be Met)
1. Study involves waste/plastic/detritus detection (macroplastic, floating waste, river litter).
2. Involves rivers, ponds, or closely related aquatic/freshwater environments.
3. Uses computer vision and/or deep learning for automated detection/classification/segmentation.
4. Reports an actual methodology or experiment (not purely conceptual/theoretical).
5. Published within the time range (early 2015 – early 2026).
6. Peer-reviewed OR reputable conference proceedings (IEEE, CVPR, etc. — conference papers explicitly included per user confirmation).

## Exclusion Criteria (Meet Any)
1. Unrelated to aquatic waste (pure marine/ocean studies without river connection; pure terrestrial/land waste without aquatic link).
2. Purely manual waste surveys or visual observation studies without any computer vision/deep learning component.
3. Opinion pieces, editorials, pure surveys without original data synthesis, or papers without reproducible methodology.
4. Papers without a computer vision component (pure chemical/physical sensing without imagery).
5. Papers outside the time range.
6. Duplicate publications (same study reported multiple times — include the most complete/version of record only).

## Notes for Agents
- The base paper (`base_published_paper/main.tex`) clearly meets all inclusion criteria.
- The reference systematic review (`base_reference_systematic_review_paper/`) is included as a contextual/state-of-the-art reference but is excluded from primary data extraction since it is a review/synthesis, not an original experiment (unless it provides unique comparative data not in original sources).
- All papers extracted into `extraction_table/` must have these criteria noted (e.g., `Included` or `Excluded` with reason field).
